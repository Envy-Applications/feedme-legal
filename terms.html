4.5 Allergies, intolerances and dietary rules. Filters, “free-from” tags, “avoid” lists and ingredient information in Feed Me are convenience tools that are worked out automatically from the ingredient lists of recipes. Scans of barcodes, photos, labels and restaurant items are not checked against your avoid list, and the ingredient and additive notes on scanned products come from Open Food Facts data, which can be incomplete. They are not a guarantee or certification that a recipe or product is free from any allergen or suitable for any medical, religious or ethical diet (for example gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, vegan, halal or kosher). They can miss ingredients, and they cannot detect cross-contamination or changes to a recipe or product. If you have a food allergy or a medical dietary need, always check the label and the full ingredient list yourself, and ask the manufacturer or restaurant, before you eat.

4.6 Product scores and ingredient information. Product health scores, additive and ingredient notes and the sources shown with them are informational summaries based on published research and regulatory information. 4.8 Eating disorders and body image. Tracking numbers can be unhelpful for some people.
