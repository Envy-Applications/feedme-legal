Delete your account & data

App: Feed Me (Envy Applications, ABN 95 927 268 876)

Last updated: 29 June 2026

You can permanently delete your Feed Me account and all of the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what gets removed, and what is kept.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

If you still have Feed Me installed and can sign in, this removes your account immediately:

  1. Open Feed Me and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE when prompted, and re-authenticate if asked.

Your account and data are deleted right away — there is no waiting period to reconsider, so only do this if you are sure.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app, can’t sign in, or would simply prefer we do it for you, email us and we will delete your account for you:

Email: support@envyapplications.com

Use the subject line “Delete my Feed Me account” and send it from the email address on your account (or tell us the email you signed up with) so we can verify the request.

We will verify ownership of the account, complete the deletion, and confirm by reply. We respond to deletion requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the data we hold about you, including:

  • Your account and sign-in record (email, display name, Firebase authentication)
  • Your profile and preferences (nutrition targets, body metrics, goals, settings)
  • Your recipes and any recipe photos you uploaded
  • Your weekly meal plans and shopping lists
  • Your nutrition log, meal history, and weight log
  • Your saved cookbooks and scanned-product history
  • Your notification settings and push notification token
  • Your referral codes and subscription entitlement record

What is kept, and for how long

A small amount of data is retained where we are legally or operationally required to keep it. None of it is used to re-create your account:

Data Why it’s kept Retention
Encrypted backups Disaster recovery Purged as backups rotate, within 30 days
Crash / error reports App stability (PII-scrubbed) Up to 90 days
Subscription / purchase records Held by Apple, Google & RevenueCat for billing, tax, and refunds Per their own retention rules — we don’t control this

Heads up about subscriptions: deleting your Feed Me account does not cancel an active subscription. Manage or cancel Feed Me Pro in your Google Play (Play Store → Subscriptions) or App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) account before deleting, otherwise billing may continue through the store.
