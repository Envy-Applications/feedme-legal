**Last updated:** 5 October 2026

> **The short version.** This summary is for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.
>
> - We collect what Feed Me needs to work: your account, the body and diet details you enter, what you log (food, water, weight), photos you add, and technical data from your device.

### 2.3 Information from other sources

- **Apple and Google**, when you sign in with them: the name and email address you allow them to share.
- **Apple Health or Health Connect**, if you connect them. On iPhone, Feed Me reads the active and resting energy you burn, and writes the calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat of meals you log, with each meal’s name, to Apple Health. On Android, Feed Me reads active, total and basal energy and writes a nutrition record for each meal you log to Health Connect. These values are read on your device to keep your calorie ring accurate. They are not uploaded to our servers.
- **The app stores**, about your purchases and subscriptions.
- **Food databases**, such as Open Food Facts, when you scan a barcode.
- **Public web pages and posts**, which you ask us to import.
- **Your coach**, if you have one: targets, goals and feedback they set for you.
- **A friend**, if you redeem their referral code: we may show you the name or label attached to that code.

### 2.4 What we do not collect

We do not collect your payment card details (the app stores handle payments), your contacts, your calendar or your precise location. The App never asks for location or contacts permission, although we do receive your IP address (from which our providers work out an approximate location), your device’s region setting and your time zone. We do not read your photo library in the background: you choose each photo. The camera is used when you take a photo or scan a barcode, and the microphone only while you record a voice note. We do not record your screen or use session replay.

### 2.5 Setting up before you register

While you set up the App, before you create an account, it signs you in to a temporary account so that your answers (including the body details you enter) can be saved. The first setup screen tells you, before it asks for any body detail, that continuing means you agree to our Terms, to this Privacy Policy, and to us using your height, weight and goals to build your plan (section 3), and we record when you did. Where analytics is on (section 5.5), it starts at this point. Whether or not analytics is on, from here our subscription provider and our crash-reporting provider also use your temporary account ID. Where ad measurement is on for your device (section 6.5), AppsFlyer does too. If you finish sign-up, the temporary account becomes your account. If you do not, it is deleted automatically (see section 10).

### 2.6 Data kept on your device

The App keeps some data on your device: your sign-in session, caches of your recent entries and recipes, your conversation with the in-app assistant, your AI permission, your usage-analytics choice and your ad-measurement choice, and a small summary of your day (totals, last meal, planned meals and shopping list) that home-screen widgets read, plus a share token, your language and your theme that the iPhone share extension reads. Signing out or deleting your account clears the data tied to the account, including your ad-measurement choice, so the next person to use the device is asked afresh. Your AI permission and usage-analytics choice stay on the device.

## 5. Analytics and app performance

**5.1 Mixpanel.** We use Mixpanel, Inc. for product analytics, to see which features are used and where people get stuck. Where analytics is on (section 5.5), events start when you first tap “Get started”, before you create an account. The App sends events that record **that** an action happened, such as opening the App, finishing a setup step, signing up (and by which method), viewing a subscription screen, starting a trial, using an AI feature, sending a message to the assistant, importing a recipe, or answering a rating prompt. When you log a meal from an AI scan, the App can also send how far the number you saved differs from the AI’s estimate, as a percentage, and how the estimate was made (no calorie figures and no food name), and it sends technical timings of each AI request. The first event carries only a random device ID. Every later event is linked to your account ID (a temporary account until you finish sign-up) and carries a few details: whether you are a Pro member, whether you joined with a referral code, whether you mainly use the App for nutrition tracking or meal planning, how many reminders you have turned on and whether push is enabled. Mixpanel keeps a profile for your account ID with the same details and your sign-up date, and receives subscription events (such as starting a trial, subscribing, renewing or cancelling) from our subscription provider. Mixpanel also records the app version, device model, operating system version, screen size and approximate location (country, region and city) worked out from your IP address. Mixpanel does not store the IP address itself as a property.

**5.2 What we do not send to Mixpanel.** Food names, meal photos, anything you type, your weight or other measurements, your goals, calorie figures, health-app data, your name or email address. The only food-related number it receives is the percentage described above. We do not use session replay or automatic event capture.

**5.3 Server events.** Our servers also send Mixpanel a few events about notifications we send and about recipe imports, tied to your account ID and with no personal content. These, and the subscription events in section 5.1, do not depend on the switch in section 5.5.

**5.4 Where and for how long.** Mixpanel stores this in the United States. We keep analytics for up to 24 months.

**5.5 Your choices.** In the UK, the EU/EEA and Switzerland, and wherever we cannot tell your region, the App sends no analytics until you switch on **Me → Notifications → Usage analytics**. We rely on your consent, which you can withdraw there at any time. We decide using your device’s region setting, which may not match where you live. Because the switch is in Settings, the setup steps you complete before you find it are not recorded. Everywhere else, analytics is on by default and you can switch it off in the same place. The switch controls what the App sends from your device, and your choice is stored on the device. It does not stop the server events in section 5.3 or the subscription events in section 5.1. You can also ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your account, or object to this use, by emailing support@feed-me-app.com.

**5.6 Crash reports and performance (Sentry).** If the App crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) with the error message and stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, a short trail of recent app activity (for example network requests the App made, such as the barcode you looked up, and launch and setup steps), and your account ID. For a small sample of sessions it also sends performance timings, and Sentry receives basic session information. Sentry does not receive your name or email address. We strip request details from reports and set Sentry not to collect personal data automatically, and we do not intend to send what you type, what is on your screen or your images. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

## 6. Advertising measurement

**6.1 What and why.** We advertise Feed Me on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok. To find out which ads bring people to the App, we use AppsFlyer, an attribution service. If you opt in, AppsFlyer tells us which ad led to an install and a sign-up, and passes these events to Meta and TikTok so they can see which ads worked. Because using a food-tracking app can itself say something about a person, we ask first and we never turn this on for anyone under 18.

**6.2 What AppsFlyer receives.** If you have opted in: your device identifiers (the vendor identifier on iOS; your advertising ID if you allow tracking on iOS, or your Google advertising ID on Android), your IP address, app and operating system version, install and session times, and your account ID as a customer ID. It also receives one event from the App, that you created an account and by which method (email, Google or Apple), and, through our subscription provider RevenueCat, that you started a trial, subscribed, renewed or cancelled.

**6.3 What it never receives.** From the App, AppsFlyer never receives your name, email address, weight or other measurements, calories, food entries, goals, photos, health-app data, or anything you type. Health information from Apple Health or Health Connect is never used for advertising.

**6.4 Who else gets it.** AppsFlyer Ltd. acts for us under contract. Meta Platforms and TikTok receive the install and event data from AppsFlyer to measure and improve their ads, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.

**6.5 When it is on.**

- **Where you are decides whether we ask first.** In the UK, the EU/EEA and Switzerland, and wherever we cannot tell your device’s region, ad measurement is **off** until you switch it on under **Me → Notifications → Ad measurement**. Everywhere else it is **on** from the moment we know you are an adult, and that same switch turns it off. We do not ask about advertising while you are setting the app up, and agreeing to our Terms is not an agreement to this.
- **Never for anyone under 18**, or whose age we do not know. For them the switch is not shown, nothing we store and no regional default can turn it on, and nothing is sent to AppsFlyer or our subscription provider for advertising.
- **Region.** In the UK, the EU/EEA and Switzerland, and wherever we cannot tell your region, we also tell AppsFlyer that you have consented, in the form those laws require. We decide using your device’s region setting, which may not match where you live.
- **Withdrawing.** Switch it off at any time under **Me → Notifications → Ad measurement**. This stops new data being sent. Data already sent stays with the recipients under their own policies. If you switch it off, we also clear the AppsFlyer ID and advertising ID we had passed to our subscription provider (its record of your device’s vendor or Android ID stays until you ask us to delete it).
- **Switching it ON belongs to your signed-in session on that device.** When you sign out, the App forgets that you switched it on and clears the same two IDs, so on another device or after a reinstall it goes back to the default for your region. **Switching it OFF is kept**: a device where you have turned it off stays off, including after you sign out and sign back in.

**6.6 iOS tracking prompt.** On iPhone, Apple’s question about tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites is asked in exactly one place: when you switch ad measurement **on** yourself. We never ask it while you are setting the app up, and we never ask it on a device where measurement is simply running on the default for your region — on those devices your advertising identifier is not used at all, and attribution works through Apple’s own aggregated measurement instead. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier is not used. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**6.7 Apple Search Ads.** While ad measurement is on for your device, our subscription provider on iPhone also collects an attribution token from Apple, which tells us whether you installed Feed Me after an Apple Search Ads campaign. This does not use your advertising identifier. It follows the same regional and age rules as the rest of this section, and it stops when you switch ad measurement off.

**6.8 US privacy laws.** Under California and some other US state laws, sending this data to advertising partners may count as “sharing” (or a “sale”) of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. In the United States this is on unless you turn it off: **Me → Notifications → Ad measurement** is your opt-out, and it is also how you exercise the right to say “do not share my personal information”. You can switch it off at any time, or email us and we will do it. Turning it off is remembered on that device, including after you sign out.

## 7. Coaching

**7.1 If you are a client.** You decide whether to connect with a coach: you send a request using the coach’s code, and the coach accepts it. From then on, and for as long as you stay connected, your coach can see, with no separate switch:

- your food diary with its full nutrition breakdown and the times you logged meals, including photos of meals;
- your water intake and your weight log;
- your weekly meal plan, including the custom shopping items and pantry items stored with it, and your recipe library;
- your habits and when you complete them;
- your calorie, macro and water targets, your name, your units, your time zone and the date you last logged;
- any check-in answers you submit (check-in photos are visible only if you switch on photo sharing); and
- a “gone quiet” alert that includes your name, if you have not logged for 3, 7 or 30 days.

You cannot switch these off one at a time. Ending the connection stops all access. Three more items stay off until you switch each one on: your body statistics (height, weight, age, sex, activity level, body-fat percentage and goal), your body measurements, and your progress photos and check-in photos.

A coach can also set your targets and goals, edit your meal plan, add recipes to your library, assign habits, leave feedback on your check-ins, export your diary as a PDF, spreadsheet or text, and use the coach AI tools described in section 4. A coach cannot edit what you have logged, and does not see your email address or account details. Feed Me has no chat or direct messaging. Coaches can write feedback on check-ins and short notes with the targets they set, and send fixed nudges and sharing requests, and clients answer check-in questions in writing.

**7.2 Ending a connection.** You can end a connection at any time. The coach’s access to your data in Feed Me then stops. A coach may already have exported or copied information while connected, and what they do with it is their responsibility. Links to meal photos that a coach copied while connected may keep working until the photo is deleted. If you reconnect with the same coach, they can again see the check-ins you exchanged earlier. A record that a connection existed is kept for 180 days after it ends.

**7.3 Notes.** Coaches can keep private notes about a client. They are stored in the coach’s own account, on the coach’s behalf, and clients do not see them in the App. Deleting your account does not delete notes that are stored in a coach’s account, so to see or remove what a coach holds about you, ask the coach.

**7.4 If you are a coach.** We collect your name and account details, the clients you connect with, and the actions you take in the coaching tools. Clients you invite can see your display name. We do not check a coach’s identity, qualifications or registrations. You must handle client information as our Terms require. If you delete your account, items you added to a client’s own account, such as targets, recipe copies and habits, stay in the client’s account.

**7.5 Who is responsible for what.** Feed Me hosts the information in the App. A coach is an independent person who decides what to do with anything they copy or export, and is responsible for doing so lawfully.

**7.6 Notices.** When a coach does something that involves you, such as updating your targets, we may send you a notification. These are fixed notices that we write.
